> ### 🎯 Core Definition: What is a Counter-Theodicy?
>
> In classical theology, a theodicy is a theoretical, philosophical text written to defend God—attempting to explain away why a good and all-powerful Creator allows evil and suffering to exist.
>
> A Counter-Theodicy (written by Jim Shankle with the support of our Scholar Consultation Council and Christian friends) turns this concept upside down. It is a high-stakes response anchored by two absolute commitments:
>
> 1. **It Rejects Abstract Bookkeeping:** It completely refuses to smooth over the horrors of terminal illness with detached academic formulas or cheap transactional math. It stands directly at the clinical bedside, fully validating the doctor's outrage over biological decay.
> 2. **It Challenges the Secular Horizon:** Instead of playing defense, it proactively examines the assumption that biological static proves a silent, empty universe. Utilizing the rigorous tools of information theory, mathematical philosophy, and historic orthodox theology, a Counter-Theodicy demonstrates that unvarnished scientific data actually reveals a purposeful, rational architecture—and points directly to a sovereign Creator who entered our exact human pain to ultimately unmake it.
